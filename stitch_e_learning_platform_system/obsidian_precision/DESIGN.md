## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a dark-first, high-performance aesthetic engineered for intensive focus, technical discipline, and instructional clarity. Built to serve educational journeys—from deep technical learning tracks to multi-tiered administrative and revenue operations—the interface strikes an authoritative balance between a modern developer workspace and an editorial media platform.

The core philosophy follows an uncompromising spatial ratio: **70–80% deep black and charcoal surfaces, 15–20% neutral text and crisp containment borders, and 5–10% strategic crimson accentuation**. Black establishes the immersive environment, white guarantees typographic clarity, and red directs user action without visual noise.

The style unifies **Dark Minimalism** with technical, high-density dashboard layouts. Interfaces rely on tonal surface stepping rather than bright fills, micro-diffused dark shadows, precise 1px borders, and disciplined typography.

## Colors

The color system operates strictly in dark mode, orchestrating deep carbon layers with surgical points of red highlight.

### Tonal Foundation
- Canvas background `#050505` serves as the root viewport.
- Surface progression follows systematic stepping: `#0B0B0B` (primary layout & tables), `#0F0F0F` (standard cards and input fills), `#111111` (structural headers), and `#171717` (elevated modals, dropdowns, and flyouts).
- Borders scale from `#1B1B1B` for nested internal separators up to `#2A2A2A` for interactive form controls and secondary buttons.

### Primary Accents
- The primary red `#E50914` is reserved strictly for high-value actions: conversion CTAs, active lesson progression bars, key metric trends, and active navigation indicators.
- Secondary `#A80710` powers destructive actions and deep gradient terminators.
- Tertiary `#FF2A2A` serves exclusively as a high-visibility alert indicator or ping.
- Ambient tones `#240406` and `#3A0508` are confined to faint radial or corner gradient stops behind display headers to enrich the dark canvas without reducing legibility.

### Text Contrast Hierarchy
- Headings and vital inputs use `#F5F5F5` and pure `#FFFFFF` to ensure WCAG AAA compliance on dark layers.
- Supporting metadata, column labels, and auxiliary descriptions use `#A3A3A3`.
- Placeholder values and disabled states resolve to `#737373`.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes **Geist** for headlines, metrics, interactive labels, and data visualizations, paired with **Inter** for sustained reading contexts, course body text, and documentation.

- **Headlines & Titles**: Characterized by tight, negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) and structured vertical metrics. They establish an authoritative, technical atmosphere without compromising readability.
- **Body Text**: Uses Inter's neutral glyph construction with open counters and generous line heights (`1.55` to `1.6`) to maintain legibility across dense educational materials and code references on dark surfaces.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Geist Semibold (`600`) with positive letter-spacing (`0.04em`) is applied to metadata tags, badge labels, table headers, and timestamp captions to preserve distinction at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture operates on an 8-point spatial model mapped to a fluid 12-column grid. The maximum content width is constrained to `1440px` to maintain optimal scanning lengths across broad dashboard viewports.

### Breakpoint Matrix
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px)**: 12 columns with `24px` (`1.5rem`) gutters and `40px` (`2.5rem`) canvas margins. Sidebars remain fixed at `260px` with persistent top navigation (`68px` height). Course catalogs render in a 4-column matrix.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8 columns with `16px` (`1rem`) gutters and `32px` (`2rem`) margins. Navigation collapses to a slim icon rail (`64px`), and course card lists adapt to a 3-column or 2-column configuration.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `12px` gutters and `16px` (`1rem`) outer margins. The sidebar collapses into a slide-over modal drawer, tables reflow into discrete vertical data cards, and high-priority learning interactions lock to a sticky bottom action bar.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily via **tonal stacking** rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing the dark minimal identity.

1. **Base Tier**: `#050505` represents root canvas emptiness.
2. **Structural Tier**: `#0B0B0B` hosts grid containers, workspaces, and content tables.
3. **Card Tier**: `#0F0F0F` with a crisp `1px` border of `#232323` forms the primary unit of content containment.
4. **Interactive & Hover Tier**: `#151515` to `#161616` signals interactive availability and hover feedback.
5. **Elevated Overlays**: Modals, dropdown lists, tooltips, and floating controls step up to `#171717`, reinforced by micro-diffused ambient drop shadows:
   - *Control elevation*: `0 4px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.40)`
   - *Card & Popover elevation*: `0 12px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.55)`
   - *Modal & Flyout elevation*: `0 20px 60px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.70)`

Headers utilize a frosted glass backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) combined with an 85% alpha background (`rgba(5, 5, 5, 0.85)`) and a `1px` bottom border of `#1B1B1B`.

## Shapes

The design system uses a hierarchical nested corner radius system. Sub-elements nested within containers maintain smaller radii to preserve visual harmony:

- **Nested Controls & Small Inputs (`8px`)**: Compact buttons, badges, table actions, and chips.
- **Standard Controls (`10px`)**: Standard input fields, primary action buttons, select triggers.
- **Content Cards (`14px`–`16px`)**: Course cards, analytics panels, and dashboard widgets.
- **Overlays & Dialogs (`18px`)**: Centered modals, system drawers, and notification dialogs.
- **Outer Hero Frames (`24px`)**: Display banners and landing section wrappers.
- **Pill (Full Radius / `9999px`)**: Exclusively reserved for status indicators, micro tags, and filter pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Solid `#E50914` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `10px` radius, height `44px`. Hover state brightens to `#FF2A2A`; active state compresses slightly (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary**: Surface `#151515`, border `1px solid #2A2A2A`, text `#F5F5F5`. Hover triggers `#1A1A1A` fill and `#3A3A3A` border.
- **Destructive**: Solid `#A80710` or bordered danger `#EF4444` for irreversible actions.
- **Ghost**: Transparent fill, text `#A3A3A3`, hover background `#151515` and text `#F5F5F5`.

### Input Fields
- Heights set to `44px` with `10px` border radius.
- Background uses `#0F0F0F` with an inactive border of `1px solid #2A2A2A`.
- Placeholder text: `#737373`. Value text: `#F5F5F5`.
- **Focus State**: Border transitions to primary `#E50914` with a subtle outline glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #E50914`).

### Cards & Panels
- Background `#0F0F0F`, border `1px solid #232323`, radius `16px`.
- Interactive course cards transition via `transform: translateY(-2px)` with border color shifting to `#2E2E2E` over `150ms ease-out`.

### Status Chips & Badges
- Strict pill silhouette (`border-radius: 9999px`), height `24px`, padding `0 10px`.
- Composed of low-opacity tinted backgrounds (`12%` alpha) with high-contrast text:
  - *Success (Published/Completed)*: `#22C55E` text over `rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.12)`.
  - *Warning (Pending Review)*: `#F59E0B` text over `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`.
  - *Danger (Suspended/Failed)*: `#EF4444` text over `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`.
  - *Info (System/Draft)*: `#3B82F6` text over `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.12)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base dimension `18px`, `4px` radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- Inactive state: `#0F0F0F` background with `#2A2A2A` border.
- Selected state: Primary `#E50914` background with crisp white checkmark or inner dot.

### Specialized Educational SaaS Components
- **Progress Trackers**: Slim `4px` or `6px` tracks in `#1B1B1B` with completed fills in `#E50914` or `#22C55E`.
- **Curriculum / Lecture Lists**: Bordered rows on `#0B0B0B` with left-accent highlight bars indicating the active video lecture or reading node.
- **Metric Stat Cards**: Compact `#0F0F0F` tiles containing Geist-driven numerical values (`32px`, semibold) with secondary indicator trends.
